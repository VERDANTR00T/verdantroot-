About Verdant Root

Verdant Root was created with a singular vision — to combine clinical reasoning with artificial intelligence to provide accessible, intelligent skin insights. We believe technology should not replace dermatologists, but empower individuals with structured understanding before professional consultation.

Our system evaluates visible indicators and user-reported concerns to offer preliminary guidance rooted in dermatological research. Every insight is delivered with clarity, responsibility, and respect for medical standards.

Founder

Founded by Sumit Kadian, Verdant Root reflects a commitment to science-driven healthcare innovation. Built with a background in medical studies and a passion for intelligent systems, the platform represents an early step toward future AI-assisted clinical tools.
